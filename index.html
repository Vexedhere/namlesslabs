// questions
Good to know.

      
Short answers to the things people ask first.

    

    

      
Do I need an account?
No. Open the workspace as a guest and start chatting. Sign in with Google, GitHub or email when you want your chats saved to the cloud and available on every device.

      
Is NamelessAI really your own model?
Yes. We fine-tune an open Qwen2.5 base with LoRA and export it as a compact GGUF file. While the model's own endpoint is being brought online, NamelessAI chats are answered by a hosted model so the workspace always works.

      
Where are my chats stored?
Guest chats stay in your browser on that device. When you sign in, chats are saved to your account in the cloud, protected so that only you can read them.

      
Can I switch models in the middle of a conversation?
Yes. Choose a different tier before any message and it takes over from there, with the earlier conversation as context.

      
Can I take my conversations with me?
Yes. Export any chat as a Markdown file, ready to drop into your notes or docs.

      
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The interface is built for small screens first, with the model picker and history one tap away.
